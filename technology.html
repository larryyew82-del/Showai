EP Equipment: Data-Driven Telematics

Stop guessing about warehouse efficiency. EP Equipment utilizes advanced Battery Management Systems (BMS) and dynamic telematics to turn your forklifts into smart, connected assets that actively lower your Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).

Distributed Fleet Aggregation

Dynamically monitor your entire fleet to balance workloads, right-size inventory, and eliminate idle equipment bottlenecks across multiple sites.
